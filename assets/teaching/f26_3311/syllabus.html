Course content

Algebra is essentially the part of mathematics that deals with the formalization of mathematical analogies. We will be covering the following topics in the first term of the course: Groups and group homomorphisms, group actions, group quotients, the symmetric group and its conjugacy classes, semi-direct products, the Sylow theorems, finitely generated abelian groups and their classification using the Smith normal form, rings and ring homomorphisms, polynomial rings, ideals and quotient rings, finite fields and their classification.

Homeworks

Homeworks are an essential part of this course. They will be posted on the class webpage by Tuesday and will be due the following Wednesday evening. The first homework due date is Wednesday, Sep 09. You will upload completed assignments on Gradescope.

To make things as legible as possible, I encourage you to use LaTeX to type your homeworks. I will share the homework files on Overleaf, an online TeX platform that is fairly self-explanatory and easy to use.

No late papers will be accepted. However, you are allowed to drop one homework of your choice without it affecting your grade. If you turn in all homeworks, I will automatically drop your lowest score.

Homework solutions will be made available after the due date.

You are allowed—and encouraged—to discuss your homework assignments with other students. However, you must write up the solutions individually.

AI and its discontents

There is no sugarcoating this: LLMs have advanced to the point where they can do the homework for you (and much more!). How you choose to use this power is up to you. There are ways to use them as assistants that help you understand and organize the material better for yourself, and there are ways to use them to voluntarily atrophy your cognitive abilities. I use them myself to move quickly through references and summarize long papers, but you really have to know what you are doing to avoid being misled by irrelevant material.

It may also be helpful to remember that one purpose of learning is for you eventually to be able to create new knowledge. On a shorter timescale, the exams in this class are in person, with pen and paper and no outside assistance, so you should make sure that you are preparing yourself to do well there. I and the class TA will do their best to ensure that you have the resources to do so.
